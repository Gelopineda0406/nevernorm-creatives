our
story

never normal.

Nevernorm Creatives started with Gelo and a camera.

Gelo began as a photographer shooting sports, portraits and the streets of Manila. That work grew into producing creative shoots, running social media pages and covering behind-the-scenes for production crews. Along the way, a one-person setup turned into a team.

Today we're a Manila-based crew that takes a project from first idea to final post. A creative director and producers shape the idea, a line producer keeps the shoot on schedule, our DOP and photographers capture it, editors and a graphic artist finish it, and our social media managers get it seen.

On paper we're a bunch of chaos: one team, different people, each with a different eye. That's the point. Nothing we make looks normal, and we like it that way.
